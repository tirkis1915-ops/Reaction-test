How it works

Click the red box to start. Wait for it to turn green, then click as quickly as you can. Clicking too early resets the round.

Average human reaction time to a visual stimulus is roughly 200 to 250 milliseconds. Your score depends on your device, screen refresh rate and browser, so compare results from the same device.
